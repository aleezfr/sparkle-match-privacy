Sparkle Match - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sparkle Match is a memory matching game made for children and families. We do not ask for, collect or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, best scores, chosen card deck and sound setting) only on your own device. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no login, no chat, no social features and no in-app purchases. We do not collect names, email addresses, location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data.

Advertising

When the device is online, the app shows one small banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen, provided by Google AdMob. The app is set up as a child-directed app: only non-personalized ads suitable for general audiences are requested, and no personalized (interest-based) advertising is used. The advertising ID is not used by this app. Google AdMob may process limited technical information (such as device and connection information) to deliver and measure ads. You can read how Google handles data at policies.google.com/privacy.

When the device is offline, no ads are shown and the whole game stays playable.

Sharing

The "Challenge" feature creates a short game code. If you choose to share it, the code is sent only through the sharing app you pick (for example a messaging app). We do not receive it.

Children

This app is designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
